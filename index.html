What the levels mean for you

  • Good (0–50): Air is clean — outdoor activity fine for everyone.
  • Moderate (51–100): Unusually sensitive people should watch for symptoms.
  • Unhealthy for sensitive groups (101–150): Children, older adults, asthma/heart patients — reduce long outdoor exertion. N95 helps.
  • Unhealthy (151–200): Everyone should limit prolonged outdoor effort; use masks outdoors.
  • Very unhealthy / Hazardous (201+): Stay indoors, use purifiers/masks, avoid outdoor exercise.

About this page

Winter smog in Delhi-NCR and North India pushes AQI searches up every November. This page shows live modelled air quality (Open-Meteo, based on the Copernicus CAMS global model) for India's biggest cities — free, no login, no ads cluttering the map. City-level pages and CPCB station-level data are planned next.
