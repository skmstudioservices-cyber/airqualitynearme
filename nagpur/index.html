What this means for you in Nagpur today

Nagpur, at the geographic centre of India, deals with dust and the emissions of coal-fired power plants in the surrounding region. Summer dust storms and winter inversions both lift the AQI seasonally.

Health advice by level

  • Moderate (51–100): unusually sensitive people should watch symptoms.
  • Unhealthy for sensitive groups (101–150): children, seniors, asthma and heart patients — cut long outdoor exertion; N95 masks help.
  • Unhealthy (151–200): everyone limits prolonged outdoor activity.
